Our Learning Method

Learn: Understand the concept in simple Telugu-English explanations.

Practice: Use browser-based simulations and fictional training data.

Verify: Compare unsafe behavior with secure defensive controls.

Build: Progress toward an isolated private lab for authorized testing.

⚠️ Authorized learning only

Test only systems you own or have explicit permission to assess. These public pages are learning simulations, not real vulnerable services. Never test real websites, accounts or networks without authorization. Keep secrets and credentials out of public repositories.
