Our Training Approach

Learn: Understand the security concept before using tools.

Practice: Use browser-based simulations and intentionally designed training exercises.

Verify: Learn how to identify the weakness and confirm the defensive fix.

Build: Progress toward an isolated practice environment and your own secure applications.

⚠️ Authorized learning only

All vulnerability testing must be performed on systems you own or have explicit permission to test. Public website demonstrations in this academy are simulations. Never test real websites, accounts or networks without authorization.
